# Supperlune — art direction

Working identity: Supperlune, places for good company. The name is exploratory and has not received trademark clearance. A custom crescent-and-table SVG emblem accompanies a restrained lowercase Cormorant wordmark. Keep technical repository and hosting identifiers stable.

The visual world is an intimate hospitality editorial: warm brown-black, cream, brass, expressive high-contrast serif headlines, quiet Instrument Sans labels and cinematic authentic venue photography. The hero uses a bar interior from Realm of the 52 Remedies, with a direct source credit. No generated interior is presented as a real venue.

Customer order remains people, mood, cost. A compact planning strip leads into nine mood collections, three across on desktop, two at tablet widths, one on a narrow phone. Middle columns sit lower to give the collection a less mechanical rhythm. Each image leads into its own two venue suggestions, with 18 distinct businesses overall. Primary-mood membership, deduplication and uncertainty remain unchanged. Unknown prices say Quote needed.

Source review, CSV imports, detailed filters and research administration stay behind secondary controls. Preserve existing local data and saved IDs. System, Light and Dark remain available, with remembered preferences; the cinematic hero stays dark in both modes. Native dialogs, forms, focus visibility and reduced-motion support are part of the same design system.

Reference notes are in docs/design-references.md. The user's design toolkit is visual reference material; its embedded scripts were not executed. Website concepts are adapted to this product without copying a reference site's branding or assets.

## Immersive room carousel

Room view is the default discovery setting. Nine equal-size photographic mood selectors rotate the active atmosphere, with previous/next controls, arrow keys, Home/End, and horizontal touch swipes over the main scene. Each mood sets a full-window authentic venue backdrop, matching page palette, heading, credit and venue suggestions. Background images crossfade after loading; reduced motion removes transitions. The carousel is manual so the scene stays where the visitor leaves it. The view and mood persist independently of research and appearance preferences.

Mood grid remains an alternate setting with nine equal-size image cards, three columns on desktop. The previous staggered heights and offsets are removed. Both views use the same primary mood assignments and unique venue catalog. In Room view the carousel shows every editorial mood, including moods without matches for a revised brief; the venue section honestly reports missing matches.

## Curved deck and continuous collection section

The room selector is now an endless curved deck of equally sized photographic prints. The selected card stays centred; neighbouring moods wrap around both ends. Pointer dragging, mouse-wheel movement over the deck, touch swipes, buttons and keyboard controls rotate moods. The page scrolls normally outside the deck. Reduced-motion preferences remove the animated card movement.

One full-width collection ground begins beneath the room scene and contains planning controls, venues and footer. No room backdrop shows through gaps between these sections. Planning controls are quiet and compact; venue photography occupies large consistent frames, with names, capacity and quote status beneath. The grid remains an equal-size, nine-mood alternate view.

## Reduced interface copy

Removed the room eyebrow, edition label, mood subtitle, gesture slogan, supporting tagline, deck numbers, gathering introduction and repeated curation sentence. The room view leads with the mood name and photography; navigation, photo credits, guest/budget inputs and venue facts remain. Reduced the reserved heading height to close the gaps left by removed copy.

## Minimal header and workspace

The browsing header contains only the wordmark, Sign up / Log in and a sun/moon icon. The icon toggles resolved Light/Dark directly and has an accessible action label. Legacy system/default preferences still work until explicitly toggled. The separate workspace.html page holds Discover, Shortlist and Manage listings; keep its shared shell synchronized with index.html when editing common markup. Hosted ChatGPT sign-in returns to that workspace through the platform-owned sign-in route. The Site's owner-private access policy remains the authentication boundary; no local-storage sign-in flag or fake password form is used.
