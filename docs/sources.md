## Inspiration

[Greg Isenberg's October 19, 2026 post](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/2112252038079816082) links to [the full episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpZOU1ugODQ). Its published description outlines a niche-and-city matching business that develops a structured list, then a community and software. We adapted that idea to event sourcing; its income projections were not validated or adopted as forecasts.

## Mood curation and photography

Nine overlapping editorial moods organize discovery; a venue may appear in several. A mood is a subjective suggestion, not evidence of privacy, quality or suitability. Guest capacity, feature matching, cost and availability still follow the source data. Unknown capacities and prices stay unknown.

`dist/discovery.js` records the exact remote photo URL, descriptive alt text, source page and credit for every image. Herb & Wood supplies its space photos. CUCINA and The Prado images are labeled general venue photos, not a claim to depict a particular bookable room. MCASD credits Daniel Lang; Mingei credits The Shalom Imaginative. Fictional listings never use real venue photos; the separate demo retains credited mood inspiration above its labeled fictional entries. Remote assets can change independently of this source repository.
