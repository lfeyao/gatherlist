# Public-source San Diego starter

Checked October 20, 2026. Leads are not endorsements or confirmed offers. Public venue descriptions are summarized briefly; no images or personal profiles were copied. Listed capacities are from provider statements, not independently audited.

| Lead | Included facts | Source | Intentionally unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Herb & Wood · The Loft | San Diego; private dining space | [Official private-events page](https://www.herbandwood.com/privateevents) | Capacity, price, fees, availability |
| CUCINA urbana · Wine Wall | San Diego; wine wall seats up to 28; semi-private group spaces | [Official group-dining page](https://www.urbankitchengroup.com/group-dining/?box=2) | Full privacy, quote, fees, dates |
| The Prado at Balboa Park | San Diego; indoor and outdoor event spaces, gardens and terraces | [Official site](https://www.pradobalboa.com/) | Particular room, small-group capacity, price, dates |
| Urban Kitchen Catering | Off-site catering for 10–500; dietary preparations offered | [Group-dining page](https://www.urbankitchengroup.com/group-dining/?box=2), [caterer About page](https://www.urbankitchencatering.com/about/) | Price, fees, date and menu-specific suitability |

All four require a direct conversation to establish the final quote and availability. CUCINA's semi-private space is deliberately not tagged as a private room. No generic restaurant meal price is used as a private-event quote.

## Inspiration

[Greg Isenberg's October 19, 2026 post](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/2112252038079816082) links to [the full episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tpZOU1ugODQ). Its published description outlines a niche-and-city matching business that develops a structured list, then a community and software. We adapted that idea to event sourcing; its income projections were not validated or adopted as forecasts.
