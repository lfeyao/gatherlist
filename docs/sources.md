## Matching limits

Venue fees, food/beverage minimums, duration, tax and service fees can differ. A two-hour minimum, a drinks deposit or a general menu price is not a full three-hour event quote. The Loma Club’s stated two-hour patio minimum remains in notes until a matching-duration total is confirmed. Normal seating limits and age restrictions at cocktail venues also need confirmation for a private event. No availability dates were inferred.
